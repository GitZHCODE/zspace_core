# Reports

- Use `README.md` to locate evidence relevant to the request; do not read all reports.
- Existing reports record historical runs. Do not rewrite them during routine
  tests or update their paths as though historical measurements were rerun.
- Write new raw logs, timings and captures to ignored build/temporary output.
  Publish a concise report with inputs, method and results only when requested.
- Maintain report links when reorganizing. Put new report-generation programs
  under `scripts/`, not among results. Existing generators remain for history.
